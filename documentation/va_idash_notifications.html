🔔 iDash Notifications & Asset Watch List

User guide and architectural definition for active RFID notifications, asset watch lists, detection level classifications, and site-configured alert rules.

1. Overview & Purpose

The iDash Notifications & Asset Watch List system bridges continuous fixed RFID reader observations with proactive asset management. Instead of relying solely on periodic handheld inventory sweeps, iDash monitors background reader reads across all deployed portals (doorways, hallways, docks, and rooms) in real time.

Key capabilities include:

  • Watch List Tracking: Monitor specific high-priority, overdue, or sensitive assets by entering their asset numbers.
  • Automated Detection Levels: Real-time categorization of assets based on fixed RFID observation recency (High, Moderate, Low, Undetected).
  • Location Mismatch Detection: Automated warning when an asset is observed at a location different from its official assigned location in AssetWorx.
  • Direct Asset Master Integration: Inspect any detected asset with automatic site and search filtering with a single click.
  • Seamless Two-Way Sync: Shared storage with the Fixed Reader Live Feed (idash-watch-list) and server-side profile backups.

2. How to Create & Use Notifications

1. Accessing the Tool Click the 🔔 iDash Notifications tile on the iDash Hub (index.aspx) or navigate directly to va_notifications.aspx.
2. Adding Watched Assets Type or paste asset numbers into the Asset Watch List textarea on the left. Entries can be line-separated, comma-separated, or space-separated (e.g. 613 EE12889, 512 EE17890).
3. Checking & Evaluating Click 🔍 Check Now. The backend API (va_watchlist_api.ashx) batch-evaluates all tags in < 70ms against current inventory records and reader event streams.
4. Saving to Your Account Click 💾 Save to persist your watch list to the server under your user profile. This allows your monitored items to persist across different workstations.
💡 Live Feed Synchronization: The watch list in iDash Notifications shares the exact same browser storage as the Fixed Reader Live Feed (va_fixed_reader_live.aspx). Assets added on either screen automatically appear on the other!

3. Detection Level Definitions

Each monitored asset is classified into an actionable detection tier based on when its RFID tag was last seen by a registered fixed reader:

Detection Level Recency Window Operational Meaning & Recommended Action
🟢 High Detection ≤ 24 Hours (Today) Actively Observed: Tag was detected by one or more fixed RFID readers today. The asset is in active circulation, verified in motion, or confirmed within range of a fixed antenna portal.
🟡 Moderate Detection 1 to 7 Days Recently Observed: Asset was observed within the past week. Normal operational state for items stationary in an active clinical or departmental area.
🟠 Low Detection 8 to 30 Days Infrequent Read: Asset has not passed a fixed reader antenna in over a week. May be parked in a patient room, storage closet, or auxiliary area lacking fixed reader coverage.
⚪ Undetected / Cold > 30 Days / Never No Recent Reader Reads: The tag has not been detected by any fixed reader in over a month or has never been observed. Recommended for physical locate sweep with handheld wand (TagTeam Scan or RFID Locator).
⚠️ Location Mismatch Any Time Observed ≠ Assigned: Tag's last observed reader location (e.g. SP1-219) does not match its official assigned location in AssetWorx (e.g. SP1-Warehouse). Indicates unauthorized or unrecorded asset transit.

4. Action Toolbar & Integration Workflows

The top toolbar and table row actions provide quick handoffs into other core iDash workflows:

📥 Export to Excel

Clicking Export to Excel generates a clean, standardized CSV export containing: Asset Number, Description, Detection Level, Last Observed Time, Observed Location, Assigned Location, Location Mismatch (YES/NO), Facility Site, Status, CMR, Recent Reader, and Signal RSSI (dBm).

📋 Inspect & Open in Asset Master

Clicking 📋 Inspect on any row or Open in Asset Master for the batch opens va_asset_master.aspx with targeted pre-filters:

  • Exact Site Pre-Selection: Rather than defaulting to "All Sites", Asset Master automatically selects the specific facility of the asset being inspected (e.g. 613 Martinsburg or 512 Baltimore).
  • Automatic Status Reset: Status is set to All Statuses so items that are loaned out, stored, or turned in remain visible.
  • Scoped Server Query: The DataTable query is immediately scoped to the watched asset(s), displaying exactly the monitored items with a green filter banner.

5. Site Configuration & Future Alert Architecture

Initial Creation in Site Configuration: In addition to user-curated asset watch lists, notification policies can be centrally defined under Site Configuration (va_site_config.aspx). This enables automated background rules that monitor site-wide hardware health and movement triggers.

The following notification categories are designed to be configured per site in Site Configuration:

1. Fixed Reader Offline Alerts Alerts when any registered Zebra or CSL reader misses MQTT heartbeat check-ins for greater than 15 minutes. Automatically flags the reader on the Fixed Reader dashboard.
2. Egress / Exit Portal Triggers Define restricted perimeter antenna portals (such as ambulance bays, emergency exits, and loading docks). Triggers an immediate notification if high-risk assets cross these boundaries.
3. Cross-Site Movement Notifications Detects when an asset assigned to Station A (e.g. 613 Martinsburg) is detected by a reader belonging to Station B (e.g. 512 Baltimore). Crucial for multi-site VISN tracking.
4. Automated Email & SMS Routing Integrates with the RecipientList in Site Configuration to deliver daily summary digests or real-time SMS alerts to designated Biomedical and Logistics supervisors.
