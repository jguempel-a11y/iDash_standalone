📄 Scanner & ENNX Quick Reference Card

Dual-sided reference card designed for field scanning technicians and trainees. Sized for standard index card printing (3×5" or 4×6") to laminate and keep with scanning equipment.

💡 Tip: Print on heavy cardstock and laminate for field durability.
Side A (Front) — Handheld Scanner Workflow
📱 VA RFID SCANNING — FIELD QUICK REF
Zebra TC53 + RF40
  1. Take scanner and sled to Scanning PC.
  2. Enter pin on Scanner: 5613
  3. Open iDash, select “Yes” to sync app (MUST BE DONE AT SCANNING PC).
  4. Log in with user & password; wait until app sync is complete before leaving PC to scan locations.
  5. Scan room location tag with Bottom Trigger (Barcode) on sled.
  6. Scan room for RFID tags using Top Trigger (RFID Sweep) on sled.
  7. If updates are needed after scan: tap Asset → review items:
    Orange: Inventoried Green: Confirmed Blue: Move / Move All Pink: Locate Mode
  8. When inventory is done, click “Inventory Done” and proceed to next location, repeating steps 5–7 until finished.
  9. When finished scanning, return to Scanning PC → click “More” tab → scroll & tap “Sync App” (MUST BE DONE AT SCANNING PC).
  10. Log out of iDash.
PIN: 5613 • Sled: RF40 Source: Enter pin on Scanner.docx

📋 Printing Instructions for Field Use:
  • Click the Print Cards button above or press Ctrl+P.
  • In the print preview dialog, select standard Letter (8.5 × 11 in) paper and portrait orientation.
  • Both cards will print with dashed cutting lines. Cut along the borders and laminate back-to-back for a rigid, moisture-resistant pocket card.
  • Optionally hole-punch the top corner to attach to a lanyard or scanner holster.

🔧 Trigger & Hardware Reference

  • Bottom Trigger: 1D/2D Barcode imager. Aim at room door frame or location placard barcode.
  • Top Trigger: RFID UHF Antenna sweep. Pull and sweep room to read asset tags.
  • PIN: 5613 to unlock the Zebra TC53 screen.
  • Scanner Sled: Zebra RF40 UHF RFID sled connected via Bluetooth/eConn.

🖈 Color Codes on Scanner Screen

  • Orange: Inventoried / expected in this location.
  • Green: Confirmed found during this scan session.
  • Blue: Located elsewhere in iDash database — tap to Move or Move All.
  • Pink: Locate Mode — Geiger counter signal-strength finder.
