🔌 Adjusting Antenna Power on the Zebra Sled Reader

The iDash app lets you control the RFID antenna power level for each scanning mode independently. Lower power narrows the read range — useful in dense environments where you want to avoid reading tags from adjacent rooms. Higher power extends range for large, open spaces.

ⓘ Equipment Required
Zebra TC53 mobile computer with Zebra Sled and the iDash app. Settings are stored per-device.

Power Level Settings Explained

Power levels are set on a scale of 1–100. The app has three independent power settings, each controlling a different scanning mode:

📡 Inventory Power Level

1–100

Controls RFID read range when performing room inventory scans using the top trigger. Default: 100.

📋 Checkout Power Level

1–100

Controls read range during asset checkout workflows. Default: 100.

🔗 Associate & Edit Power Level

1–100

Controls read range when associating RFID tags to assets or editing records. Kept low intentionally to read only the tag directly in front of you. Default: 5.

ⓘ Why is Associate Power so low?
When pairing a physical RFID label to an asset record, you only want to read the one tag you are holding in front of the reader — not every tag in the room. Keeping this at 5 ensures precise, single-tag reads.

How to Change Power Levels

1
From any screen, tap the MORE tab at the top-right of the app navigation bar.
2
Scroll down the More menu to find APP SETTINGS near the bottom, then tap it.
3
Tap the power level field you want to change (e.g., Inventory Power Level).
A numeric keypad will appear. Clear the existing value and type your new number (1–100).
4
Tap the blue checkmark on the keypad (or tap done/return) to confirm the value.
Repeat for any other power levels you want to adjust.
5
Tap SAVE in the top-right corner of the App Settings screen.
You will be returned to the More menu and a "Settings updated" confirmation banner will appear at the bottom.
✅ Settings take effect immediately. New power levels apply to your next scan — no app restart required.

🎯 Recommended Power Settings

ScenarioInventory PowerNotes
Standard room inventory 100 (max) Best for most VA rooms. Reads all tags in a standard patient room without needing to get very close.
Dense environments (hallways, storage rooms packed with equipment) 50–70 Reduces bleed into adjacent areas. You may need to sweep more slowly or in closer passes.
Tag association / pairing N/A Always use the Associate & Edit Power Level (keep at 5) — ensures only the single tag directly in front of the reader is picked up.
Large open bays or warehouses 100 Full power. Walk the space in a grid pattern to ensure full coverage.

💡 Tips & Notes

  • Inventory Duration (also in App Settings, default 7) controls how many seconds the scanner actively reads per trigger squeeze. Increase it for slower sweeps; decrease it if you want faster per-location scans.
  • Lower power ≠ fewer reads. In most VA environments, power 70–100 reads the same tags. The difference becomes noticeable in rooms sharing thin walls with adjacent tagged inventory.
  • Settings are per-device. If multiple TC53 units are in use, each must be configured individually in App Settings.
  • Do not set Associate power above 10 unless specifically troubleshooting a pairing issue. Higher values will read multiple tags simultaneously, making single-tag association unreliable.
