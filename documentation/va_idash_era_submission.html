Executive Assessment Scope Statement:
This Enterprise Risk Analysis Report (ERAR) covers the operation and network connection of the iDash Enterprise Suite (running on Microsoft Windows Server 2019 / 2022, Microsoft SQL Server 2019 / 2022 database, Zebra MC3330xR Android 11–14 wireless RFID & barcode mobile terminals, Zebra FX9600 / FXR90 fixed passive RFID readers with BSP firmware v3.30.x, and Zebra ZT411R / ZD621R RFID label printers running Link-OS v7.4) within the VA Enterprise network. The overall residual risk to the VA Enterprise is Moderate.

Data Sensitivity & Patient Privacy: iDash exclusively tracks capital medical equipment, surgical instrumentation trays, RME assets, manufacturer serial numbers, RFID electronic product codes (EPCs), and physical room locations. Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) is created, stored, processed, or transmitted by the system.

☑ 9. Actionable ATO / ERA Submission Gathering Checklist

To complete the ERA submission package and initiate the VA Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) review for an Authority to Operate (ATO) or Authority to Connect (ATC), the following facility-specific information and artifacts must be compiled:

1

VA Service Now RITM Initiation

The facility Biomedical Engineering department or SPS Business Owner must open an official Service Now request under the Specialized Device Security Division (SDSD / SDCD) queue requesting an Enterprise Risk Assessment for iDash (Intelligent Distributed Asset Scanning Hub).

2

Facility & Stakeholder Contacts

Identify and confirm the designated roles for the submission cover sheet:
• Facility Information System Security Officer (ISSO): Name, email, and phone.
• Biomedical Engineering POC: Lead HTM / Biomedical Engineer overseeing SPS tracking.
• SPS Business Owner: Chief of Sterile Processing Service or designee.
• Vendor Contact: ID Integration, Inc. (Gary Moe / John Guempel).

3

Server Host & Database Environment Specifications

Document the exact target server provisioning details:
• Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN) and Static IPv4 Address of the iDash host VM.
• Microsoft Windows Server edition (2019 Standard or 2022 Datacenter).
• SQL Server instance name, collation, and database authentication mode.
• Hypervisor host platform (VMware ESXi cluster / Microsoft Hyper-V).

4

Peripheral Hardware MAC Addresses & Serial Numbers

Compile a facility hardware asset spreadsheet containing:
• Serial numbers and MAC addresses for all Zebra MC3330xR mobile terminals.
• Static IP addresses, MAC addresses, and physical installation locations for all Zebra FX9600 and FXR90 fixed readers.
• Network print queue names, IP addresses, and serial numbers for Zebra ZT411R and ZD621R label printers.

5

Network Port Validation & Boundary ACLs

Provide the local VA OIT Network Operations team with the Appendix B Ports, Protocols and Services (PPS) table to establish the SPS-OT VLAN, verify that Port 80/443, Port 8181, Port 1433, Port 5160, Port 6101, and Port 1883/8883 are open internally within the enclave, and confirm no outbound Internet traffic is routed.

6

NIST FIPS 140-2 Validation Certificates

Attach the formal NIST cryptographic validation certificates to the submission dossier:
• Zebra MC3330xR Cryptographic Module: NIST FIPS 140-2 Certificate #3866.
• Zebra Link-OS Printer Cryptographic Module: NIST FIPS Certificate #4767.

7

Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) Entry

Coordinate with the boundary ISSO to register the standard SPS-OT POA&M items in the VA Enterprise GRC tool for the Moderate Risk residual determination (covering periodic audit log reviews under AU-6, and firmware lifecycle monitoring for Zebra fixed appliances under CM-2).

8

Local Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Sign-off

Ensure the facility SPS department has an approved Standard Operating Procedure covering routine asset inventory scanning, autoclave tray barcoding, and daily scanner charging/reconciliation workflows.

Summary & Submission Strategy:
By adhering strictly to this ID Integration Inc. / iDash baseline, the package eliminates the legacy printer end-of-life findings (ZD500R/ZT410R) present in older submissions. This positions iDash for an expedited Moderate Risk determination with minimal POA&M overhead, streamlining the transition to an Authority to Connect (ATC) and formal Authority to Operate (ATO).
