2. Resolution of Primary Blocker: EPPlus → ClosedXML Migration

EPPlus versions 5 and later are distributed under the Polyform Noncommercial license, which prohibits commercial and non-evaluative enterprise use without purchasing proprietary commercial licenses per developer or deployment. To guarantee that iDash is 100% legally unencumbered and free of any commercial licensing obligations across all VA hospital sites and regional deployments, EPPlus has been replaced across the board with ClosedXML.

Why ClosedXML?

  • Permissive MIT License: Free for commercial, government, educational, and enterprise use with no license fees, subscription requirements, or seat restrictions.
  • Native OpenXML Foundation: Built directly on top of Microsoft's official DocumentFormat.OpenXml SDK, ensuring total compatibility with Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  • Drop-in Feature Parity: Fully supports styled headers, custom cell formatting, numeric-precision preservation, formulas, worksheet range scanning, and column autofit.

5. CDN Dependency & Integrity Control Policy

iDash is designed for deployment both on connected enterprise regional servers and on mobile, intermittently connected RFID carts operating inside VA medical facilities. To guarantee reliability, security, and consistent rendering across both environments, the following policy applies:

  • Version Pinning: All CDN script tags must reference explicit, immutable version numbers (e.g., jquery-3.7.1.min.js, chart.js@4.4.0, datatables.net/2.0.8) rather than unpinned or @latest tags.
  • Offline Mobile Cart Operation: For environments where RFID carts operate on isolated networks without outbound public internet access, UI bundles are backed by locally hosted fallback copies located in Assets/ and js/, ensuring complete offline autonomy.
  • Subresource Integrity (SRI): Production release manifests recommend the inclusion of cryptographic integrity="sha384-..." attributes on all externally referenced CDN assets to guard against third-party tampering.
