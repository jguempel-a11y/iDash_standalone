📋 Post-Scan: ENNX Export & Email

After returning from the field, generate and email the Equipment Not in Expected Location (ENNX) report for the day’s scan session using the Intelligent Distributed Asset Scanning Hub.

When to use this guide. Follow these steps immediately after completing your room inventory scans and syncing the scanner app at the Scanning PC. This sends the daily ENNX report to the configured email recipients.
📄 Printable Pocket Card: Need a laminated cheat-sheet at the Scanning PC? See the Scanner & ENNX Quick Reference Card (Side B).

💻 Phase 1: Access the Hub
1 Go to the Scanning PC & Open Edge
Return to the Scanning PC — the same computer where you synced the scanner before heading out. Open Microsoft Edge.
⚠ Must be done at the Scanning PC. The ENNX tool reads scan data that was uploaded when you synced the scanner. Use the same PC that was used for the pre-scan sync.
2 Open the VA Intelligence Hub
Click the VA Intelligence Hub bookmark in Edge, or navigate directly to the URL below.
🔗 Bookmarked URL:
http://visn5-8.tail03df01.ts.net/idash/va_ennx.aspx
💡 Shortcut: The VA Intelligence Hub link is already saved in Edge Favorites on the Scanning PC. Look for it in the Favorites Bar or press Ctrl+Shift+B to show the Favorites Bar.
3 Sign In
Enter your username and password. These are the same credentials you use to log in to the iDash scanner app.

iDash login screen — use the same VA username and password as the scanner app.

📊 Phase 2: Build & Send the ENNX Report
4 Click “ENNX Export & History”
After logging in you will land on the Hub dashboard. Click the ENNX Export & History tile or navigate directly to the ENNX page. The ENNX Export tool will open with date and filter controls.

ENNX Export page — you will set the Date, Site, User, and Tagged Items Only checkbox in the next steps before clicking Build ENNX.

5–8 Set the Report Filters
Fill in the four filter fields as shown. All four are required for an accurate daily ENNX report.

📅 Step 5 — From Date

Enter today’s date (the date the room scans were performed). The format is mm/dd/yyyy. Leave To Date blank to cover the full day.

🏢 Step 6 — Site

Select 613 from the Site dropdown. This filters results to your facility’s assets only.

👤 Step 7 — User

Select your username from the User dropdown. Only users who have scan sessions for the selected date will appear in this list. If your name is missing, verify the scanner sync was completed.

✅ Step 8 — Tagged Items Only

Check the Tagged Items Only checkbox. This limits the export to assets that have RFID tags applied, which is the standard for daily inventory reports.

Example of correctly filled-in filters: From Date = scan date, Site = 613, User = your username, Tagged Items Only checked. The green banner at the top confirms the email was sent (Step 10).

⚠ User not in dropdown? If your username does not appear in the User list, the scanner sync did not complete successfully. Return to the scanner, go to MORE → Sync App, wait for confirmation, then reload this page.
9 Click “Build ENNX”
Click the Build ENNX button. The tool will query the database and populate the ENNX Text Preview box and the Data Details grid below it.

After clicking Build ENNX — the ENNX Text Preview fills in with location and asset IDs, and the Data Details table shows each asset with its current and previous location. Review to confirm the data looks correct.

💡 Review the data before emailing. Scan the Data Details grid to confirm assets and locations look as expected for today’s scan session. If something looks wrong, check your filter settings.
10 Click “Email ENNX + Excel”
Click Email ENNX + Excel to send the report. Email recipients are pre-configured — you do not need to enter any email addresses.

Success — the green banner “Email sent successfully to 1 recipients.” confirms the ENNX report and Excel file have been delivered. Your post-scan workflow is complete.

✓ Done! The ENNX report and Excel export have been emailed to the configured recipients. No further action is needed for today’s scan session.
