🔒 Accessing iDash — VA Asset Intelligence Hub

How to sign in to iDash, navigate the hub, and understand what each section contains.

What is iDash? iDash is the VA Asset Intelligence Hub — a web-based portal for tracking, reporting, and managing RFID-tagged VA equipment. It provides access to asset reports, ENNX export tools, scanning dashboards, guides, and administrative tools from any network-connected computer.
🔒 Step 1: Sign In
1 Open the iDash URL
Open any web browser and navigate to the iDash URL provided by your site coordinator.
The sign-in page will load showing the VA Asset Intelligence Hub sign-in card with a Username and Password field and a Sign In button.

The iDash sign-in page — enter your site number as the username and your assigned password, then click Sign In.

🔑 Sign-In Credentials — Site 613 Martinsburg Example
Username613  —  your VA site number
Password6131234  —  provided by your site administrator
ⓘ Your credentials are specific to your site. The username is always your site number (e.g., 512, 613, 668). Your site administrator will provide your password. If you do not know your credentials, contact your iDash site administrator.
2 Click Login
After entering your username and password, click the Login button. You will be redirected to the VA Asset Intelligence Hub home page.
💡 Tip: If you see an error after clicking Login, verify that your username contains no spaces and matches your site number exactly. Passwords are case-sensitive.
🏠 Step 2: The VA Asset Intelligence Hub
3 The Hub Home Page
After login you arrive at the VA Asset Intelligence Hub. The header confirms your login with “Signed in as 613” and shows the server connection status in the top right.

The Hub home page after signing in as site 613. Note the “Signed in as 613” confirmation in the top-left header.

The header bar contains three important controls:
Header ControlWhat It Does
★ Support RequestSubmit a support ticket or question directly from the hub.
☀ Light / DarkToggles the hub between light mode and dark mode. Your preference is saved per browser.

A Welcome card appears on first visit, summarising what the hub does:
  • Search Assets — find any asset or see where it was last scanned
  • Reports — view inventory progress, tagging activity, and export data
  • Guides & Downloads — step-by-step procedures and approved software
  • ⭐ Favorites — click the star on any tile to pin it for fast access
  • The 🔒 Admin Tools section requires a separate admin login — most users will not need it
Click “Got it — don’t show this again” to dismiss the welcome card permanently on this browser.

🚪 Step 4: Signing Out
6 Always Sign Out on Shared Computers
Click Sign Out in the top-right corner of the hub when you are finished. This ends your session and returns to the login screen.
⚠ Shared computers: iDash sessions stay active in the browser until you sign out or the session expires. On a shared or public workstation, always click Sign Out before stepping away. Do not simply close the browser tab.
💡 Bookmark tip: Once signed in, bookmark the hub URL (/iDash/documentation/index.aspx) for quick future access — the hub will remember your session and skip the login screen until it expires.

🔧 Troubleshooting Sign-In Issues

IssueSolution
Login button does nothing or page reloads blankVerify your username (site number only, no spaces) and password are correct. Try a different browser.
“Invalid username or password” errorCheck for extra spaces before or after your username. Passwords are case-sensitive. Contact your site administrator if correct credentials still fail.
Page times out or shows a server errorVerify you are connected to the VA network or MiFi. The iDash server must be reachable from your workstation.
Hub shows no tiles after loginTry refreshing the page (F5). If tiles remain missing, your account may not have the correct site assignment — contact your administrator.
Session expired / redirected back to loginSessions expire after a period of inactivity. Simply log in again — your work is not lost.
