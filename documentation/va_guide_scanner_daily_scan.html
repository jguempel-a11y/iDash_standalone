📱 Daily Scanner Workflow

Complete end-to-end procedure for using the Zebra TC53 + RF40 sled to perform room inventory scans with AssetWorx.

Scope of this guide. Covers the full daily scanning procedure: unlocking the scanner, syncing the app at the Scanning PC, logging in, scanning room locations and RFID assets, reviewing color-coded results, taking action on unexpected items, and syncing data back when finished.

💻 Phase 1: Setup at the Scanning PC
1 Bring Scanner & Sled to the Scanning PC
Before leaving to scan rooms, complete all setup steps at the Scanning PC while connected to the network. The app must sync data before you head out.
📍 Why this matters: The app download from the server includes all current asset records and locations. Scanning without a fresh sync means the scanner has stale data and inventory results may not be accurate.
2 Enter PIN on the Scanner
Wake the TC53 scanner and enter the PIN to unlock it.
5 6 1 3

Scanner unlock PIN — enter this on the device keypad to unlock the screen.

💡 Tip: If the scanner is in a holster or charging cradle, press the power button first to wake the screen before entering the PIN.
3 Open AssetWorx and Sync
From the home screen, tap the AssetWorx icon to launch the app. When prompted, select YES to sync with the master database.

TC53 home screen — tap the AssetWorx icon to launch.

Sync prompt — always tap YES when at the Scanning PC to download the latest data.

⚠ This step MUST be done at the Scanning PC. The sync connects to the VA network server. If you are away from the PC / off-network, the app will not be able to reach the server.
4 Log In and Wait for Sync to Complete
Enter your username and password, then tap Login. A progress spinner will appear while the app downloads the latest asset data. Do not leave the Scanning PC until sync is 100% complete.

Login screen — enter your VA credentials and tap Login.

Sync in progress — wait for this spinner to fully complete before leaving the PC.

Sync Completed — only leave the PC once you see this confirmation at the bottom of the screen.

⚠ Do not leave the Scanning PC until you see “Sync Completed.” Leaving early means the scanner has incomplete data and scans will not be reliable.
📡 Phase 2: Scanning Room Locations
5 Scan the Room Location Tag
Once sync is complete and you are at the room to scan, use the bottom trigger on the RF40 sled to scan the location barcode tag posted on or near the room door.

▼ Bottom Trigger — Barcode

Scans 1D/2D barcodes. Point at the room location tag barcode and squeeze the bottom trigger to read it. The room name and number will appear in the app header.

▲ Top Trigger — RFID

Activates the RFID antenna. Hold the sled toward the room and squeeze the top trigger to detect RFID tags on equipment in the room.

Scan Room Location — the room list is empty until you scan the location barcode tag. After scanning, the room will load and you can begin reading RFID tags.

6 Scan RFID Assets in the Room
With the room loaded, use the top trigger to sweep the sled around the room and read all RFID tags on equipment. The asset list will populate as tags are detected.

Orange items = assets expected in this room that have not yet been read by the RFID sled. Sweep around the room until all orange items turn another color.

As you scan: ✓ Green = Inventoried  📍 Blue = Unexpected location  ■ Orange = Not yet read

✓ Green — Inventoried (found in expected location) 📍 Blue — Unexpected location (found here but belongs elsewhere) ■ Orange — Expected but not yet scanned
7 Review Results and Take Action
After sweeping the room, review the color-coded asset list. For items requiring action, tap the item to bring up the Action menu.

Mixed results: orange = still unread (keep scanning), blue = found here but assigned elsewhere.

Action menu — tap any item to see available actions for that asset.

8 Tap “Inventory Done” and Move to Next Room
When you are finished reviewing and acting on all items in the room, tap the Inventory Done button in the top-right corner. Then repeat Steps 5–8 for each remaining room.

Inventory Done button (top right) — tap when you have finished reviewing all assets in this room. This marks the room as inventoried and returns you to the location scanner screen.

💡 Repeat for each room: Return to the Scan Room tab, scan the next room’s barcode tag, sweep for RFID tags, review items, and tap Inventory Done. Continue until all locations are complete.
💻 Phase 3: Return to Scanning PC & Sync
9 Return to the Scanning PC — Sync App
When all rooms have been inventoried, return to the Scanning PC. Tap the MORE tab on the scanner, scroll down, and select SYNC APP.

MORE tab — scroll down and tap SYNC APP to upload all scan data to the server. This must be done at the Scanning PC while on the VA network.

⚠ SYNC APP must be done at the Scanning PC. This step uploads all your scan results to the server. If you sync away from the PC / off-network, the upload will fail and your scan session data will not be saved.
10 Log Out
After sync is confirmed complete, scroll to the bottom of the MORE tab and tap LOG OUT.
💡 Always log out when finished. Logging out protects your credentials and ensures the next user starts with a fresh, clean session.
