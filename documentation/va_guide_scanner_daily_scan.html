📱 Daily Scanner Workflow

Complete end-to-end procedure for using the Zebra TC53 / TC530e with RFID Sled to perform room inventory scans with iDash Standalone.

Scope of this guide. Covers the daily operational procedure in iDash Standalone: authenticating operator sessions, loading room manifests, performing RFID/barcode sweeps, reviewing color-coded asset matches, resolving misplaced assets, and committing results to the iDash database.
💻 Phase 1: Authentication & Room Loading
1 Operator Sign-In on the TC53
Open the iDash Inventory web application (/va_inventory.aspx). Sign in with your assigned site operator credentials to lock your session to your facility.

Technician Sign In — populates operator profile and locks site assignment.

Ready state — scanner armed and ready to load room location.

2 Scan Room Location Tag
Aim at the room door location tag and squeeze the bottom trigger (1D/2D barcode imager) to load the room manifest.

▼ Bottom Trigger — Barcode

Scans 1D/2D barcodes. Use this for room location door tags and individual equipment barcode labels.

▲ Top Trigger — RFID

Activates the RFID antenna sled. Hold toward the room and sweep to detect all RFID tags in range.

Room loaded — expected asset manifest is preloaded into local memory.

📡 Phase 2: Live Scanning & Review
3 Sweep Room with Top Trigger (RFID)
Walk around the room sweeping with the top trigger. Tags are detected automatically and matched against the loaded room manifest in real time.

Live sweep — green rows show verified items; blue rows highlight items belonging elsewhere.

✓ Green — Verified In-Place (Expected & Found) 📍 Blue — Misplaced (Belongs to another room) ■ Orange — Expected but Not Yet Read
💾 Phase 3: Resolution & Commit
4 Commit Room Scans
Tap Commit Scans. If misplaced assets were found in this location, choose how to record them:

Commit dialog — choose whether to move misplaced assets to this room.

Scans committed — records saved to database; ready for next room.
