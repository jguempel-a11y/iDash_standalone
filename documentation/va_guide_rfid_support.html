📞 VISN 5 RFID Technical Support

Quick Reference Guide — how to get help, who to contact, and how to escalate. VISN 5 Enterprise RFID Asset Awareness support is provided by Team VIT (ID Integration + Impres Technology).

Fast, reliable support keeps VISN 5 RFID operations running smoothly. This guide walks you through exactly how to get help — from opening a ticket to closing it out — plus who to contact if you need to escalate.
📄 Technical Support Process
1
Open a Ticket
Email VA RFID Support and the Team VIT Project Manager with a brief description of the issue to open a support request.
✉ varfid_support@id-integration.com ✉ bheishman@imprestechnology.com
2
Discovery Call
Team VIT will respond within 48 hours of ticket submission to schedule a discovery call and determine the root cause of the issue.
⏱ Response SLA: 48 hours from ticket submission to discovery call scheduling.
3
Resolution
Work with the Team VIT support team to implement and validate the resolution. Ensure the fix is confirmed working in your environment before closing.
4
Ticket Closure
Team VIT will reply to the original ticket email thread confirming the resolution. This reply serves as the official record of closure.
📄 Keep the email thread intact. Always reply-all to the original ticket thread rather than starting a new email. This keeps the full history in one place for audit purposes.
▲ Escalation Path
If an issue is not resolved within an appropriate timeframe or requires additional attention, escalate in the order below. Move to the next level only if the current level does not respond or resolve the issue within the expected timeframe.
