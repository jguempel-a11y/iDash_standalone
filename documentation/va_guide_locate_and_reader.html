📍 Guide: Locate Feature & Reader Settings

How to use the RFID "Locate" feature to find specific assets, adjust reader antenna power, handle missing location tags, and properly log out of the scanner.

This guide covers 4 topics: Locate mode, antenna power adjustment, missing location tags, and the scanner logout procedure. All procedures use the Zebra TC53 with RFID sled.

Source: VISN 5 Use and Configuration Guide for AssetWorx! 4.0.8.7, Sections VI–IX (pages 32–44)

📍 Using the "Locate" Feature
Use when: You need to find a specific asset that you know is somewhere nearby but can't see it. The Locate feature turns the reader into a proximity detector.
1 Open Locate Mode
From the AssetWorx! main menu, tap "Locate". This switches the reader from bulk-scan mode to single-asset proximity mode.
2 Enter the Asset to Find
Enter the asset tag ID or asset name in the search field. You can also scan a barcode to look up the asset. The reader will now search only for this specific tag.
3 Walk and Listen
Hold the trigger and walk around the area. The reader works like a Geiger counter:
  • Far away — Slow beeping, low signal bar
  • Getting closer — Beeping speeds up, signal bar grows
  • Very close — Rapid beeping, near-full signal bar
  • Found it — Continuous tone, full signal
The screen shows a signal strength meter that fills as you approach the tag. Walk slowly and rotate the sled to pinpoint the exact direction.
💡 Tip: Lower the antenna power when using Locate mode (see Adjusting Power below). Lower power = shorter range = more precise locating. Start with the lowest setting and increase if needed.
⚡ Adjusting Reader Antenna Power
Use when: You need to change the scan range — wider for inventory sweeps, narrower for precision locating.
1 Open Power Settings
On the Zebra sled reader, the power adjustment is in the AssetWorx! Settings menu. Tap the gear icon and look for "Antenna Power" or "RF Power Level".
2 Choose Your Power Level
The power level is measured in dBm (decibels-milliwatts). Adjust the slider or select a preset:
LevelRangeBest For
Low (5–10 dBm)~1–3 feetPrecision locating, dense tag environments
Medium (15–20 dBm)~5–10 feetRoom-level scanning, shelves and cabinets
High (25–30 dBm)~15–25 feetFull room sweeps, large open areas
⚠ Higher is not always better. High power reads tags from adjacent rooms, corridors, and through walls — this creates false positives (assets that appear to be in the room but are actually next door). Use the lowest power that covers your target area.
🚨 Missing Location Tag: What Do I Do?
Situation: You arrive at a room and the RFID location tag on the wall is missing, damaged, or not scanning. Here's how to handle it.
1 Try to Re-Scan the Area
Before reporting a missing tag:
  • Check if the tag has fallen off the wall — look on the floor or behind furniture near the doorframe
  • Try scanning from multiple angles and distances
  • Inspect the tag for physical damage (torn, wet, peeled off)
2 Manually Select the Location
If the location tag truly cannot be scanned, you can manually select the location instead:
  1. In the AssetWorx! app, tap the location search field
  2. Type the room number or location code (e.g. 1B-123)
  3. Select the correct location from the search results
  4. Proceed with your scan as normal
3 Report the Missing Tag
After completing your scan, report the missing location tag so a replacement can be installed:
  • Note the room number and building
  • Report to your team lead or submit an iDash Support Request from the hub
  • Include whether the tag is missing entirely, physically damaged, or present but not scanning
💡 Tip: Tags that are present but not scanning may have a dead RFID chip. These need replacement even though the paper label looks fine.
🔒 Logging Out of the Scanner
Always log out when you're done scanning or handing the device to another user. This ensures scan sessions are properly attributed to the correct user.
1 Sync Before Logging Out
Always sync your data before logging out. If you're in Batch Mode, upload all pending sessions first. Unsent data remains tied to your login — the next user won't be able to send it.
2 Tap Logout
From the AssetWorx! main menu:
  1. Tap the menu icon (hamburger or three-dot menu) in the top corner
  2. Select "Logout"
  3. Confirm the logout when prompted
  4. The app returns to the login screen
3 Return or Hand Off the Device
After logging out:
  • Return the scanner to the charging cradle
  • If handing to another user, they can log in with their own credentials
  • The sled reader can be left attached — no need to disconnect
⚠ Security: Do NOT leave the app logged in when the device is unattended. Another user's scans would be recorded under your name, creating inaccurate audit trails.
